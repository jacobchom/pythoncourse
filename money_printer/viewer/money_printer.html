How the trick works

  1. Wind the pusher all the way to the front. Lift the lid and lay three notes flat on the tray, edge to edge, with the first one against the exit slot. Put the lid back.
  2. Push a note-sized piece of paper into the front slot. Fins on the lid force it down a ramp into a pocket under the tray.
  3. Turn the crank wheel about 6½ times. The screw moves the pusher, which shoves the whole row of notes along one note-width, so the front note slides out of the back slot.
  4. Stop as soon as the note is out; the next one is waiting flush with the slot. Repeat for notes two and three. The last one stops 2 mm short, so pull it out.
  5. Both slots are at the same height (14.2 to 17.0 mm), so it looks like one sheet going straight through.
  6. To reset, wind the wheel back about 19 turns, lift the lid and take the papers out from under the tray.

The 3-note base and lid need a bed at least 210 × 280 mm. A 2-note version (200 × 195 mm) fits a 220 × 220 bed.

Assembly: push the two screw parts together (the keyed peg only fits one way, which keeps the thread lined up). Drop the tray onto its rails, spin the carriage onto the screw from the back end, lower the screw into the wall notches and press the lid on. No glue, rods or rubber bands.
