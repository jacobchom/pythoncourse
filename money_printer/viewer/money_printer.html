How the trick works

  1. Lift the lid and lay a note on the hidden tray, pushed back against the exit slot. Put the lid back.
  2. Push a note-sized piece of paper into the front slot. Inside, fins on the lid force it down a ramp into a pocket under the tray.
  3. Turn the crank wheel about 6½ times. The lead screw drives a nut carriage, and its thin pusher bar slides the note out of the back slot.
  4. Both slots are at exactly the same height (14.2 to 17.0 mm), so it looks like one sheet going straight through.
  5. To reset, turn the wheel the other way, lift the lid and take the paper out from under the tray.
